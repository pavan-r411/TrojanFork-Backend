## Slide 1 — Title
**TrojanPlate**  
Your goals. Your campus. Your plate.  
AI-powered daily diet planning for USC students

---

## Slide 2 — Problem
Between classes, Trojans must juggle:
- Dining hall menus + meal swipes  
- Nearby restaurants + budget  
- Diet, allergies, and nutrition goals  

Generic diet apps ignore what’s actually available. Restaurant apps don’t plan the day.

---

## Slide 3 — Solution
Two modes. One profile. One day plan.
1. **USC Dining** — Village, EVK, Parkside  
2. **Off-Campus** — walkable restaurants (0.3 mi default)  

Up to **three** recommendations per meal, with explanations. Swap a meal → the rest of the day updates.

---

## Slide 4 — Demo story
1. Onboard (goals + diet)  
2. USC Dining day plan from real hall menus  
3. Swap lunch  
4. Off-Campus vegetarian under $12 within 0.3 mi  

---

## Slide 5 — AI & safety
- Plans use **verified menu snapshots**  
- Never invent dishes, prices, or allergen safety  
- Unknown nutrition labeled unavailable  
- Allergies and hard budgets always win  

---

## Slide 6 — Feasibility
- FastAPI + snapshot data + mobile web  
- Ready for SG / Hospitality pilot  
- Live menu refresh is a stretch — not required for value  

---

## Slide 7 — Ask / impact
Help Trojans eat toward their goals with food they can actually get today.
